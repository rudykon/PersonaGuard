01 —THE RESEARCH QUESTION

Make every personalized
action accountable.

A model can estimate a profile. Deciding whether to add sensors, adjust an interface, or retain that profile calls for different evidence.

PersonaGuard treats each proposed use as a separate audit route. It records the evidence, its evaluated scope, and the rule that connects it to an action. When support is incomplete, the decision carries that boundary forward.

I.

Match the scope

Tie claims to the people, settings, and information conditions actually evaluated.

II.

Justify the increment

Compare added sensing or adaptation with the relevant evaluated alternative.

III.

Keep the decision trace

Make permissions, constraints, and requests for more evidence inspectable.
