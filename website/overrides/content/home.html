Research projectFrom Evidence to Action: Auditing Personalization Decisions in HCI Systems

01 / THE QUESTION

A better estimate. A justified action?

In the MER-PS data we analyse, participants watch videos and use a joystick to report how pleasant and activated they feel. Correcting a report’s timing may improve a measurement. Whether to personalize, collect more information, or retain a profile requires its own evidence.

OUTPUTNext action + reason + review condition

The reference is built from other people’s reports; it is not a ground truth for this person’s feelings.
