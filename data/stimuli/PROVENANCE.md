# REFED stimulus provenance

The 15 files under `media/REFED_video_clip/` were obtained through a link
provided by REFED corresponding author Ziyu Jia on 2026-08-13. They are local
research inputs and are excluded from Git; they must not be redistributed by
this project.

The authoritative local filenames and receipt-time SHA-256 digests are stored
in `configs/stimuli/refed_15_videos.csv`. A digest match certifies that an
experiment used the received files unchanged. It does not independently
resolve the approximately 1.7--2.8 second difference between MP4 duration and
the released label/physiology segment length. Content experiments therefore
report a fixed alignment-offset sensitivity analysis rather than choosing an
offset from outer-held-out labels.
